TATA STEEL SAFETY PROCEDURES

PPE REQUIREMENTS - FURNACE AREA:
- Heat-resistant gloves
- Face shield
- Fire-resistant jacket
- Safety boots
- Hard hat

PPE REQUIREMENTS - GENERAL SHOPFLOOR:
- Safety helmet
- Safety shoes
- High visibility vest
- Safety glasses

LOCKOUT-TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Shut down the equipment completely
Step 2: Isolate all energy sources
Step 3: Apply lockout and tagout devices
Step 4: Verify zero energy condition
Step 5: Wear required PPE before starting work
NOTE: Never begin maintenance until all energy 
sources are verified as isolated.

EMERGENCY EVACUATION PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Activate nearest fire alarm
Step 2: Proceed to nearest emergency exit
Step 3: Do not use elevators
Step 4: Assemble at designated muster point
Step 5: Report to safety officer for headcount

FIRE SAFETY:
- Know location of nearest fire extinguisher
- Do not block fire exits at any time
- Report any fire hazard immediately to supervisor
